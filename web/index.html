Ask about a token

This adapter was trained to report its own next-token prediction (and a logit-lens reading of its layer 12, 18 or 24) for one token of a passage. Paste a passage, name one of its tokens, pick a question and build it, then press Send: the first token of the reply is the answer. Entropy questions answer with a digit, 0 (almost certain) to 7 (very uncertain).

Steering (σ)

layers to
Adds α·σ·direction to the residual stream at each layer of the band. Past about ±3σ the model breaks into repetition. Double-click a slider to zero it. "Where" also gates the neuron pushes and the head ablation: reasoning only stops at the end of the thinking, answer only starts there (with thinking off: the prompt, or the reply).

Neurons (× own scale)

Adds a push to one neuron's activation at every token. A neuron's scale is its largest activation over a text sample. A push of 0 only watches it: the tint below shows its activation on each token of the reply (blue below zero, ochre above, its own scale fills the colour).

Attention heads (ablate)

Removes heads from the attention layers. An ablated head's output is replaced by its average on ordinary text, or by zero. The ranking is how much each head helps the model copy a repeated passage of random tokens (induction). No single head matters much, so ablate the top few dozen together. "Where" gates this too.

Read-out now (z)

Live, per token, averaged over the band, with steering in effect. A steered emotion's own meter partly shows the added vector itself; the other meters show how the model responds.
